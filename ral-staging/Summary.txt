Summary of multimedia material

The video (100 s) shows (1) the staging problem: robots wait at docks and an
adversary places a burst of alarms; (2) a Gazebo shift with fixed staging
that the adversary has observed versus a shift with leakage-aware
randomized staging; (3) why per-dock probabilities do not determine the
value (Lemma 2 example); (4) when randomization pays as the observation
probability q grows, closed-loop simulation results and the limits of the
model; and (5) concurrent three-robot Nav2 execution in Gazebo, showing that
planning on navigation-verified reachability realizes the planned value.

The code archive contains the exact solvers, the simulator, the Gazebo/Nav2
setup and all raw results needed to reproduce every number and figure in
the paper.
